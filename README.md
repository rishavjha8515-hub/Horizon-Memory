# Horizon Memory

[The project tries to give a visual representation  of qubit decoherence to the user using a real and imaginary number graph showing the plotting of k vs phase functions.]

## The Physics

[This stimulation is based on my research into quantum decoherence near black holes specifically Reissner - Nordstrom black hole which has two horizons and both Mass and Charge.

In the stimulation,qubit's decoherence, te spiralling complexx-plane trajectory,and the eventual collapse are all computed directly from the formulas in my paper.]

## Built With

-HTML5 Canvas
-Vanilla Javascript

## How to Play

- Watch your qubit fall toward the black hole - coherence decays as surface gravity (k) increases.
- Click "Measure" to slow your fall, aat the cost of permanently lowering your maximum possible coherence
- Reach zero coherence, and the qubit collapses.
